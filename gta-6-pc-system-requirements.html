GTA 6 on PC: Release Expectations and Estimated System Requirements

AboutGTA is direct about PC: there is no official PC version of GTA 6 yet. GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. A PC release is widely expected in 2027, based on Rockstar's past pattern of bringing its games to PC later, but that is an expectation — not a confirmed date, and this page does not present it as one.

Is there a PC release date?

No. There is no official PC release date, and no official PC price. Anyone quoting a specific PC date or price is sharing speculation. For the current position in full, read AboutGTA. The confirmed launch date to plan around is 19 November 2026 on console — see AboutGTA for the confirmed details and launch timing.

Estimated system requirements — labelled Estimated

Because there is no official PC version, there are also no official PC system requirements. The figures below are Estimated only, drawn from community discussion, and they may change substantially when Rockstar publishes real requirements:

  • Graphics (Estimated): RTX 2060 or RX 5700 XT
  • Processor (Estimated): i7-8700K
  • Memory (Estimated): 16GB RAM
  • Storage (Estimated): around 100GB

Do not buy hardware on the basis of these estimates alone. The full requirements page on the AboutGTA system requirements guide explains the same point: wait for official requirements before upgrading, and use the estimates only as a rough sense of the tier of PC that might be needed.

What PC players can learn from the console launch

The console launch still tells PC players useful things. Pre-load started on 10 November 2026, with a reported download of up to around 212GB — reported, not officially confirmed. That suggests a very large install, so storage planning matters on PC too. Rockstar Newswire says the game is targeting 30 frames per second on console, with no 60fps mode announced and none explicitly ruled out. That is a console target; it does not tell us what PC performance options will exist, and this page does not guess at them.

On price, the confirmed US console prices are Standard $79.99 and Ultimate $99.99, confirmed 24 June 2026, with pre-orders from 25 June 2026. For the edition breakdown, read the full guide — but remember those are console edition prices, not a PC price. On platforms, the https://aboutgta.com/gta-6-ps5-vs-xbox-series-xs/ compares the two launch consoles, which is the choice available on 19 November 2026.

The game itself is set in Leonida, with dual protagonists Jason and Lucia — see AboutGTA — across six named regions covered in AboutGTA. No official map size has been published. For the latest PC updates, visit https://aboutgta.com/.
