GTA 6 Release Date: 19 November 2026

AboutGTA tracks the GTA 6 launch from the confirmed date outward. Grand Theft Auto VI launches on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That is the confirmed launch date, and it is the date every other launch-week plan in this hub is built around.

What launches on 19 November

The launch covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See the GTA 6 release date guide for the confirmed date and platforms. A PC version has not been officially confirmed, so it is not part of the 19 November launch. PC players should read AboutGTA for the current position: widely expected in 2027, but still unconfirmed. Treating a 2027 PC date as confirmed would be a mistake, and this hub does not do that.

Pre-load started on 10 November 2026, giving console players time to download before launch day. The reported download size is up to around 212GB. That figure is reported, not officially confirmed, so players planning storage should leave headroom above it rather than treating it as exact. For launch-day timing, the https://aboutgta.com/gta-6-release-time/ explains when the game goes live.

Price and editions at launch

At launch, the standard edition costs $79.99 in the US and the Ultimate edition costs $99.99. Both prices were confirmed on 24 June 2026, and pre-orders opened on 25 June 2026. If you have not chosen an edition yet, read the full guide on AboutGTA before deciding — it covers what the Ultimate edition adds and whether the extra cost is worth it for you.

Performance: targeting 30fps

Rockstar Newswire says GTA 6 is targeting 30 frames per second. That is the official wording, and it is important not to overstate it. Targeting 30fps is not the same as a locked 30fps, and no 60fps performance mode has been announced. At the same time, a 60fps mode has not been explicitly ruled out. Until Rockstar says more, the honest summary is: 30fps target, performance modes unconfirmed.

Launch week does not end on launch day

Two days after launch, on Saturday 21 November 2026, Rockstar and PlayStation present GTA 6: The Festival at Mana Wynwood in Miami. It is a one-night live music event with Travis Scott, Cardi B, Don Toliver and Kodak Black on the lineup. Lottery entries closed at 02:00 PKT on 11 November 2026, and winners can buy on DICE from 22:00 PKT on 11 November 2026, up to two tickets each, non-transferable, ages 17 and over. DICE lists $60 — a DICE-listed price, not a Rockstar-announced price. Full details are on AboutGTA.

The launch also sets the stage for exploring Leonida. The story follows dual protagonists Jason and Lucia, explained on AboutGTA, and the world spans six named regions including Vice City and the Leonida Keys. For the map itself, read the full guide — no official map size has been published.
